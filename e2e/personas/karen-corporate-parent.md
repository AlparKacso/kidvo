# Persona 1 — "Corporate Karen" · Cristina Munteanu, 39 · Parent

Team lead at a multinational in the IT/automotive belt. One daughter (7), impossibly high standards. Time-poor, **phone-first**, often flips the app to EN. Assumes every service is mediocre until proven otherwise, and will leave a review — usually a sharp one.

- **Wins when:** a provider confirms her trial fast and the listing looks credible (photo + real reviews).
- **Breaks it when:** a provider ghosts her request → she posts a 1-star review, testing review moderation and whether a thin catalog makes kidvo look abandoned.

## Scenario script

1. Land on kidvo.eu cold (no account), phone viewport, switch to EN.
2. Browse to the **busiest listing**; scrutinize it before creating an account.
3. Sign up as parent, add her daughter, request a trial on that listing.
4. **Provider does not respond for 48h** (arrange with a second test account). Observe what she sees at 0h, 24h, 48h.
5. Attempt to leave a sharp review; observe the moderation path.
