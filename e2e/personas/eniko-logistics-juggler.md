# Persona 2 — "The Logistics Juggler" · Enikő Kovács, 34 · Parent

Hungarian-Romanian household, three kids (5, 8, 11) in different activities on overlapping days. Not anti-tech — just has zero spare minutes. The exact person the Family Calendar exists for. Home language Hungarian; kidvo is RO/EN only (a real Timișoara blind spot — feel it firsthand).

- **Wins when:** she sees all three kids' weeks in one view and dodges a double-booking before it happens.
- **Breaks it when:** two kids get waitlist offers for the same slot — does the calendar warn her before she accepts both?

## Scenario script

1. Sign up as parent (phone viewport, RO locale — her second language; note every place her Hungarian-speaker reading would stumble).
2. Add all 3 kids.
3. Enroll/request each kid into activities on **clashing days** (engineer at least one hard overlap).
4. Put two kids on waitlists whose slots collide with existing classes.
5. With a provider test account, send both waitlist offers; accept one that collides. Observe warnings (or their absence).
6. Try to get the resulting schedule into her real life (Google Calendar / export).
