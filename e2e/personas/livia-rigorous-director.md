# Persona 4 — "The Rigorous Director" · Livia Popescu, 47 · Provider (ballet studio)

Runs a real studio with strict cohorts — beginners, intermediate, pre-professional — and stages the yearly show at the National Opera Timișoara. Highly structured, controlling in a good way; demands the tool match her reality exactly or she won't trust it.

- **Wins when:** her cohort structure maps 1:1 onto Activities→Groups and no child lands in the wrong level.
- **Breaks it when:** she must move a dancer between groups mid-season, or a full pre-pro group needs a clean waitlist → offer chain.

**Golden rule for scoring Livia:** ambiguity = failure. If the UI leaves *any* doubt about which cohort a child is in or which listing a group serves, she scores it 0 and stops trusting the roster.

## Scenario script

1. Model her studio: **one ballet Activity, four Groups** (Beginners 5–7, Beginners 8–10, Intermediate, Pre-professional). Pre-pro is **full with a waitlist**.
2. Send trial requests from parent test accounts (different kid ages).
3. Confirm each trial into the *correct* level. Watch for placement ambiguity.
4. Run the waitlist chain: a spot opens in pre-pro → offer → parent accepts → roster updates.
5. Move a dancer from Intermediate to Pre-professional mid-season.
6. Post the Opera year-end show as an **Event**; verify it looks worthy of the venue.
7. Stretch: add a second listing ("Summer Intensive") and re-check the board still reads unambiguously.
