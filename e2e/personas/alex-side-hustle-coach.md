# Persona 5 — "The Side-Hustle Coach" · Alex Șerban, 28 · Provider (swim/football)

Solo operator, Instagram-native, currently runs bookings out of DMs and a spreadsheet. Tech-fluent and growth-obsessed. Cares about two things: filling empty spots and paying no commission. Tier-1 target and toughest churn risk.

- **Wins when:** kidvo fills a spot he couldn't fill himself, or the waitlist auto-offers save him DMs.
- **Breaks it when:** a week passes with views but zero trial requests → he decides IG is enough and churns.

**Golden rule for scoring Alex:** he compares everything to Instagram. Any flow slower than a DM, any number less motivating than his IG insights, scores at most 1.

## Scenario script

1. Self-serve signup → **two listings** (swim + football), each with 1–2 groups. Time it; he multi-tasks and abandons anything slow.
2. Find and use the share templates to push both listings to his channels.
3. Simulate low-volume traffic: a few views, one contact reveal, one trial request over a "week".
4. Fill the swim group, build a small waitlist, then free a spot and watch the waitlist→offer engine work (or make him do the DMs anyway).
5. Open the dashboard cold, as if between training sessions: does the funnel keep him?
