# Persona 3 — "The Artsy Maestro" · Rareș, 54 · Provider (guitar & piano)

Wonderful with kids, runs lessons from a small studio, tracks everything in a **paper notebook**. Barely uses email; lives on WhatsApp and phone calls. The least tech-savvy provider — the make-or-break usability test. Phone only.

- **Wins when:** he lists once, does nothing else, and a parent still reaches him by phone.
- **Breaks it when:** he's asked to write listing copy or grasp "publish a group" — where the GTM promise ("I'll write the listing for you") gets tested.

**Golden rule for scoring Rareș:** if an item requires him to understand the Activity/Group distinction *at all*, it can never score 2. The model must be invisible to him.

## Scenario script

1. Hand him a phone and his notebook. Signup → live Activity, **zero help. Time it.**
2. During the 24h approval window, observe what he believes is happening.
3. With a parent test account, send a trial request; watch him find and answer it.
4. Lure test: he sees the badge on the **Groups** nav item and taps it. Watch what happens. *(Do not rescue him.)*
5. Recovery test: if he ends up somewhere confusing, can he get back to safety alone?
